Humans Have Reached Mars

The Starship rocket successfully landed on the Red Planet this morning.

The mission marks a significant milestone in human space exploration. After a 115-day-long journey, the crew of 12 finally arrived at their destination. This is the first time humans have set foot on a planet other than Earth.
